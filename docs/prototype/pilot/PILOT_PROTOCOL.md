# Astraea Seven-Day Pilot Protocol — Draft

## Purpose

Observe whether new players understand Astraea's character setup, real-life Life Quest rewards, Function analysis, and the Prepared Deck. This pilot is for usability learning, not for validating balance or health outcomes.

## Before recruitment

The product owner must decide participant count, age/eligibility, consent language, data retention and deletion owner, feedback/interview method, success thresholds, and the decision rule for the next development scope. Do not recruit until these decisions and the privacy notice have been reviewed.

## Data boundary

Prototype measurement is optional, off by default, stored locally, and has no upload/export path. It records only allowlisted gameplay milestone identifiers and bounded result/content-version values. Do not collect real names, contact details, free-form notes, quest evidence, health data, or interview recordings in the app. Keep interview notes outside this event store under an approved research process.

## Seven-day outline

1. Introduce the prototype and obtain informed consent before use.
2. Let each participant create a character, complete a Life Quest, read the story excerpt, choose a deck, and try the Function tutorial.
3. Ask participants to explain what Life Progress represents, whether any prompt felt pressuring, and what happened when they interrupted LockTarget.
4. Invite voluntary use over seven days; do not require daily action or punish inactivity.
5. At day seven, conduct a short interview using the approved method, then honor data deletion/retention commitments.
6. Review observations against owner-approved thresholds and record a dated scope decision separately from this implementation status.

## Limitations

This protocol is not evidence that a pilot occurred. M3 Training conversion and the integrated M7 battle/post-battle journey are not implemented, so this draft cannot yet validate those experiences. Never claim M8 pilot validation until a human-run pilot is completed and results are recorded separately from this code repository.
